Blesc pitched at the United Nations University (UNU).

Children’s mental health is a challenge shared around the world, not only in Japan. We presented Blesc around one question: how can the adults around a child notice small changes the child can’t put into words?

Blesc is a school journaling app that detects changes in students’ short daily entries and notifies teachers with the reason. Teachers never read the entries, and the AI does not diagnose or assess risk. We explained our design: protect students’ privacy and leave the final judgment to people.

We were glad to introduce Blesc to people working on global challenges, and we thank everyone who gave us this opportunity.
