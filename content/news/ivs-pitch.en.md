Blesc pitched at IVS, one of Japan’s largest startup conferences.

We talked about three walls schools face today. Teachers usually notice changes only after something visible has happened. In surveys, students pick the “safe” answer. And teachers’ time is limited.

Blesc picks up differences from each student’s own usual in short daily entries, and notifies teachers with the reason only when something changes. Because teachers never read the entries, students can write in their own words. Together with Blesc for Teachers, where teachers write too, we aim to help whole schools notice changes in one another.

Presenting to founders and investors was a valuable experience. We’ll put the tough questions and encouragement we received into building the business.
