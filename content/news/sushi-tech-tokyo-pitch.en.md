Blesc pitched at SusHi Tech Tokyo, the innovation conference hosted by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government.

We started from one question: how can teachers notice small changes in students who answer “I’m fine” when asked “Are you okay?” Students write a short daily entry, and teachers are notified, with the reason, only when the writing changes significantly from that student’s own usual. Teachers never read the entries, and the AI neither diagnoses nor judges. We shared our belief that AI should help people notice, not judge people.

We’re grateful for the chance to present on such a big stage. The questions and feedback we received will shape what we build next.
