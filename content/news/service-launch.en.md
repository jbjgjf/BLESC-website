Blesc, a school journaling app that helps teachers notice changes in their students, is now available. It comes in two versions: Blesc for Students and Blesc for Teachers.

### Why we built it

Changes in how a student feels often begin before they can be put into words. Asked “Are you okay?”, most students say “I’m fine.” Periodic surveys can’t show what’s normal for each student, so small changes are easy to miss. And teachers look after a whole class while juggling lessons, club activities and paperwork. Blesc was built to close that gap.

### How it works

1. **Everyone writes.** Every student writes a short entry each day during a few minutes of homeroom: study, clubs, friends, future plans, anything.
2. **AI asks one question.** Based on the entry, the AI replies with a single short question. Answering is optional.
3. **Teachers hear about changes.** Only when a student’s writing changes significantly compared with their own past entries does the teacher receive a notice, along with the reason.

### What we care about

**Teachers never read the diaries.** Teachers receive only the class, student number, time and the basis for the change. Knowing no one reads their entries lets students write honestly.

**No diagnosis, no risk scores.** Blesc only reports that something has changed. What it means, and whether to reach out, is up to the teacher who knows the student.

**Teachers write too.** With Blesc for Teachers, teachers write during the same homeroom time. Administrators receive only change notices and their basis, just as with students.
