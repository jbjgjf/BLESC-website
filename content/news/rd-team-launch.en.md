Blesc has formed two R&D teams that combine psychology and AI, and we are looking for university and industry partners to research with us.

### Why an R&D team

Blesc detects “differences from usual” in students’ writing and explains them to teachers. When working with students’ words, “it probably works” isn’t good enough. How should change be measured accurately? How should the reason be explained so teachers find it convincing? We set up dedicated research teams to answer these questions through research as we build.

### What we’ll work on

- More accurate ways to detect change in students’ language
- Ways to explain detected changes that teachers find convincing
- Testing Blesc’s effectiveness through studies in schools

We aim to share results through peer-reviewed papers and international conferences. Blesc covers the research costs.

### Partners wanted

We’d especially like to work with people in:

- Adolescent mental health and developmental psychology
- Natural language processing and computational social science
- Educational technology and school psychology

We’re flexible about how we collaborate, from discussing research topics to advising on study design to co-authoring papers. Contact: uryu@blesc.jp
